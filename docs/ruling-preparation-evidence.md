# Evidence retained when preparing a ruling

[Prepare a ruling](claim-adjudication.md#prepare-a-ruling) gives the five steps. These requirements still apply to each case.

- Start with a neutral case file and the [dossier brief](ruling-dossier-brief.md). Fetch and read the source at the relevant base and head, name the supported promise and the consequence, and save probes, counterevidence and search limits. Run `ruling_dossier.py` for candidates. A missing search or unresolved fact cannot be hidden by confidence.
- Give blind assessors the case, pinned rule and [precedent sheet](adjudication-record.md#the-sheet-of-earlier-rulings), without the recommendation. Preserve their exact briefs and outputs. Record their model, family, effort, exposure, answer, confidence and reason. Follow the record's independence requirements. In the issue #48 session two sessions of one model agreed on 28 of 30 labels and the owner then changed about seven; another session alone is not evidence of correctness.
- Prepare a possible new problem beside a credit question. When a comment describes nothing beyond the known problem, say so. When it might become a new problem, prepare its candidate dossier and [impact card and blind label](impact-calibration.md#assign-an-impact-band) before asking. Show credit, eligibility and band together so the owner loads the case once. New problems and labels remain owner decisions.
- Put the facts and every party's pick, confidence and reason inside the question. Ask in plain language, one ruling at a time. Group decisions about one pull request so its context is explained once. If any part needs the owner, show the whole case with any settled parts marked. Save the question and verbatim answer immediately. Check that an immediate reversal does not rest on a misreading before recording it.
- When a ruling sets or changes a rule sentence, put the exact proposed sentence in the option. A sentence inferred afterwards is not an adopted rule. Preserve the owner's ground verbatim, or record that none was given. Keep reversed first answers unchanged and record the miss and actual fix. State what a new label or outcome will select and what readers should infer before asking the owner to label cases.

The detailed contracts for [confidence](adjudication-record.md#confidence), [saved-ruling checks](impact-calibration.md#recheck-a-saved-ruling), [impact](impact-calibration.md) and [audit reconciliation](evaluator-audit.md#reconcile) remain in force. No preparation step authorizes a paid dispatch or a wider delegation. The current v2 policy is an inactive proposal until its actual adoption receipt exists.
