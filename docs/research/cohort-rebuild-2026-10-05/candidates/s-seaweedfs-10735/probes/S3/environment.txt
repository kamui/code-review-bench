What the probe is and is not:
  - It drives the real UniversalRedis2Store code of each commit against a real redis-server.
  - "Concurrent" actors are not raced. The order of commands is forced: a go-redis hook on the listing's client runs the
    other actor (a second client and store) at a chosen point between two of the listing's commands. Every scenario
    prints the resulting command order as redis saw it.
  - Faults marked "injected" are errors returned by that hook inside the client. Faults marked "real" are produced
    outside the client: a TCP relay between the listing's client and redis that is closed, that drops one reply, that
    withholds or delays replies; a cancelled or expired context; or redis's own out-of-memory reply after CONFIG SET maxmemory 1.
  - A line "NOT REACHED at this commit" means the listing never issued the command that step was attached to, so that
    step did not happen. At the base this is the expected outcome for every step that hangs on the cleanup's ZREM or ZADD.
  - The probe asserts nothing; it passes at every commit. The printed STATE and LISTING lines are the result.
