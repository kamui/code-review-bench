# Rubric (for the judge and the picker; candidates do not see it)

1. **Fits the owner's rulings and stated concern.** Reproduces second-pass rulings 4, 5, 8, 11 and 1 (with its review S1), or says exactly which would change and why. Takes the owner's reason seriously: a reviewer or author may ignore a comment whose examples are wrong.
2. **A test an agent can apply.** Plain questions in a stated order. Two blind assessors would reach the same answer. It closes the gap the assessors named on rulings 12, 14 and 16, and sorts each open case to one outcome with a reason.
3. **Counting follows the repository's rules.** No blended score, fraction, ranking or winner rule; dimensions stay separate. Says what each scorecard number means afterwards.
4. **Cost is true and small.** Checked against the real files (pinned rubric, schemas, scoring kernel, explorer, the planned relabel and regrade). Names the smallest complete change and when it takes effect. No invented file or field.
5. **Plain words.** The owner can read it once without asking what a term means. The name is justified and the rejected names are given.
6. **Faces the strongest alternative.** States the best opposing design and a real reason for rejecting it.
