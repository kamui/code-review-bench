# Why this proposal

I chose a checked record at the point of asking because the failures happened in several places. Better dossiers alone would miss the recording session, reviews of old rulings, and questions about whether a comment catches a family. The current check is worth keeping, but its passing cases show that it checks declarations rather than source work.

I considered these alternatives:

- **Add more instructions to the brief.** The handbook was missed after the earlier contract lesson. More prose would leave the same failure path open. Keep the search guidance, then require evidence of its use.
- **Require every contract search to be rerun by two agents.** This costs repeated investigation even when a pinned source is sufficient. Have the asking session reopen the decisive sources and contrary evidence. Repeat searches when coverage or applicability is doubtful.
- **Build a comprehensive adjudication platform now.** Issue 59 needs broader schemas, backfill, summaries and a shadow stage. The immediate need is to preserve fresh predictions for the 13 pending questions. A paired record beside the existing receipt is enough to begin.
- **Patch v3 until it agrees with every saved label.** That would hide overfitting. S9's blind agreement already depended on a clause written from S9. Preserve versions, distinguish adopted decisions from proposed generalizations, and test on new cases.
- **Make every public call with users promised.** S9 disproves that shortcut. Documentation can establish a promise without users, as R5 shows; users cannot alone establish every use of a public name.
- **Make every undocumented use without users advice.** This would lose explicit promises and deliberate support, including R2's bare-name source command. Practice is one basis for a promise, not a universal prerequisite.
- **Let unanimous high confidence trigger delegation.** The saved record includes confident agreement against the owner. It also mixes model families, repeated cases and incomplete evidence. Preserve the existing delegation conditions and learn which dimensions of confidence predict agreement before widening authority.
- **Encode all semantics in the checker.** The checker can verify records, source pins and declared referral states. It cannot decide whether documentation really governs an operation or whether two cases share the relevant shape. Claiming otherwise would repeat the original mistake with a passing check attached.

The proposal adds more structure than another checklist paragraph, but keeps it beside existing dossiers and receipts. Its main tradeoff is immediate recording work for a record that can later show whether less owner involvement is justified.

Verification was local and read-only against the supplied repository. I read the discussion first, inspected the requested rules and receipts, ran the dossier checker across nine directories, ran its four unit tests, and exercised three inadequate inputs in temporary directories under this working directory. All temporary test directories were removed. I ran no model, grading command, regrade, upstream workload, or network search. The proposed schema and validator extensions are specifications, not implemented or tested changes.
