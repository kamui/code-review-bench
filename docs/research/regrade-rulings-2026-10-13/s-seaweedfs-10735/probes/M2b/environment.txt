  zz_probe_m2_test.go, ../run.sh
      One probe for both windows of the logical-expiry branch. It drives the real redis2 store, and in cases E, F

      The file under test is "logically expired with its value still in Redis": creation time 10 s ago, TtlSec 5,

    windows are one Redis round trip wide (between two consecutive commands of one listing).

  - The S3 gateway and the HTTP upload handler were not raced; cases E and F call Filer.CreateEntry, which those
    handlers call.
