Notes on the M1 probe runs (2026-10-12)

How to repeat: clone grpc/grpc-go four times under one scratch directory as run.sh describes, copy
m1_probe_test.go and m1_documented_way_test.go into the root of each clone, apply the two patches to the
two patched clones, unpack a Go module cache, then run run.sh and run-documented-way.sh.

Two things about these runs that a reader should know:

1. A first version of the widened-window patch slept inside updateAddrs, between its Unlock and its `go`
   statement. That run showed nothing: a policy calls UpdateAddresses from inside its own callback, and
   gRPC delivers state updates to a policy one at a time, so the policy was told IDLE only after
   updateAddrs had returned. The run was discarded. The patch saved here delays the start of the new
   attempt instead, and results/base-widened-window.txt is from that patch.

2. run.sh was edited while it ran (only the comment above step 5). The shell then reported a syntax error
   at its last line, after all six steps had finished and written their outputs. The outputs are complete.

"sockets" in the result lines is the number of open connections channelz lists under the sub-channel.
"re-resolutions requested" counts calls to the resolver's ResolveNow after the empty update. A CONNECTING
sub-connection whose attempt is cancelled asks for one whatever the new list holds (see
TestM1ControlNonEmptyWhileConnecting). In one of 1,600 rounds that started in TRANSIENT_FAILURE the count
was 1: the request made by the failed attempt before the update reached the resolver after the count
had been read.
