# First answers of the admission round

One folder per candidate. `recommender.json` is the asking session's first answer. It was committed before any blind session ran. `answers-sol.json`, `answers-astra.json` and the two `notes-*.md` files are the blind sessions' own files, copied without a change.

Every blind session was given the brief as its prompt and three files, and nothing else of the round: [`brief-admission.md`](brief-admission.md), [`rule-admission.v1.md`](rule-admission.v1.md) as `rule.md`, one case file, and the sheet of earlier rulings for that case as `earlier-rulings.md`. Each sheet is the candidate sheet of `ruling_record.py precedents` without the ruling the case comes from, so that no session reads the owner's words on its own case.

[`brief-admission.md`](brief-admission.md) is the [blind assessor brief](../../../blind-assessor-brief.md) with one new part, "Admission". The shared brief has no part for this decision type. The owner has not read the new part.

## The later questions

The folders `GT-j5`, `GT-j7` and `GT-y3` hold the first answers on the three reconciliation cases. Those sessions were given [`brief-candidates.md`](brief-candidates.md) and version 9 of the two questions as `rule.md`.

The folders `OP-candidate-j-1`, `OP-candidate-j-2`, `OP-candidate-p-1` and `OP-candidate-q-1` hold the first answers on the four later admissions, and `OP-grouping-j` those on the grouping. Those sessions were given [`brief-admission.md`](brief-admission.md) or [`brief-grouping.md`](brief-grouping.md), and [`rule-admission.v2.md`](rule-admission.v2.md) as `rule.md`: the test the owner chose on 2026-10-13, as the asking session reports it, over the rule text in force.

`recommender-label.json` in each candidate's folder is the asking session's proposed label, in the form of an answer. The one blind label session read the nine blinded cards under [`../impact/inspection/cards/`](../impact/inspection/cards/), with [`brief-labels.md`](brief-labels.md) as its prompt and [`rule-labels-older.v1.md`](rule-labels-older.v1.md) as `rule.md`. Its answers and notes are [`../impact/inspection/answers-sol.json`](../impact/inspection/answers-sol.json) and [`../impact/inspection/notes-sol.md`](../impact/inspection/notes-sol.md), copied without a change.

The owner then changed the labelling sentence, as the asking session reports. `recommender-label.v2.json` is the asking session's second proposed label, under the new sentence. A second blind session read the second versions of the nine cards under [`../impact/inspection/cards.v2/`](../impact/inspection/cards.v2/), with [`brief-labels.v2.md`](brief-labels.v2.md) as its prompt and [`rule-labels-pre-existing.v1.md`](rule-labels-pre-existing.v1.md) as `rule.md`. The brief differs from the first in four sentences: it names the cards' subject as the rule does, and it no longer tells the session to ask the boundary's test question or to name an exception, because the rule says neither applies. Its answers and notes are [`../impact/inspection/answers-sol.v2.json`](../impact/inspection/answers-sol.v2.json) and [`../impact/inspection/notes-sol.v2.md`](../impact/inspection/notes-sol.v2.md), copied without a change.
